The comparison study's near-parity findings concern its tested models and teacher settings. The Fisher connection is a local second-order approximation, Nash scheduling uses a local separable progress model, and Road's gradient constraints do not guarantee downstream retention. These qualifications are retained in the canonical evidence notes rather than turning reported gains into general claims.
