# October 24: Thirty Detailed Studies and Reusable Comparisons

This update publishes a focused mechanism and experimental-evidence audit. It is not a fresh exhaustive search of all catalog entries.

## Catalog changes

The total is **146 records**: **67** strict multi-teacher on-policy, **52** offline, **15** adjacent/alternative, **3** single-teacher foundations, and **9** reviews/tutorials.

Added [MergeDistill](https://aclanthology.org/2021.findings-acl.254/), an NLP antecedent that maps cached masked-token predictions into a union vocabulary. Its first-public date is June 5, 2021; it is a backfill, not a new 2026 paper.

MT-SDPO moved from strict MOPD to adjacent feedback-mediated self-distillation. Its external experts provide answer-eligible feedback, while one privileged EMA self-teacher supplies the direct probability target. Eligibility and lexical sanitization do not certify feedback correctness. See the [primary method, §§4.1–4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.02548v1).

Fourteen existing catalog records received targeted corrections or additional qualifications: MT-SDPO, PromptSD, Open-MOPD, TrustMOPD, ROAD, RGBM, Rethinking, D3-MOPD, UCMOPD, DN-MOPD, MOPD-Router, Latent-MOPD, SF-MOPD and SoFT. Detailed evidence for the broader 30-study set is published separately. Existing first-public dates were preserved.

The current v2 name is UCMOPD; UP-MOPD is a historical alias. D3's independently selected benchmark peaks differ from one deployable checkpoint. Reward recomputation does not refresh histories. Frozen calibration constants differ from dynamic weights; partial projection differs from guaranteed conflict removal; configured training cost differs from full-pipeline speedup. The detailed cards retain these distinctions and original source locations.

## Selection and evidence limits

The 30 comprise **12 offline/NLP antecedents**, **14 direct MOPD methods or comparative analyses**, and **4 adjacent cases**. Reviews and single-teacher foundations are not counted toward the 30. Selection prioritizes relevant mechanism contrasts and empirical controls; it does not imply 30 independent algorithms or complete coverage.

Coding is AI-assisted, with primary-source versions, section/table locators and interpretation limits. Results are attributed to their papers; they have not been independently reproduced. Cross-paper connections are our synthesis. There was no independent human double coding, and these selected cases do not estimate field-wide frequencies.

No citation increase, external endorsement or acceptance by another Awesome index is claimed. Public additions are research resources; no survey manuscript is published in this update.
