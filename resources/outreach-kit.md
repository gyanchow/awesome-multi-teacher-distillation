## Hugging Face Collection draft: eight-paper reading path

Manifest: **Prepared**. Account and Collection publication: **Deferred by owner**; no Collection has been created or published by this task. The eight paper IDs and their order match the [English guide](https://github.com/gyanchow/awesome-multi-teacher-distillation/blob/main/resources/guide.md) and [Chinese guide](https://github.com/gyanchow/awesome-multi-teacher-distillation/blob/main/resources/guide_zh-CN.md). All eight Hugging Face paper destinations were verified by paper ID and title on 2026-10-05. PMOPD's page and public paper API both returned HTTP 200 after the search browser could not retrieve it. This verifies destinations, not independent replication of findings. Notes draw on existing catalog evidence, not the automatically generated summaries on some HF pages.

**Collection title:** `Multi-Teacher Distillation: An 8-Paper Reading Path`

**Short description:** `From classical and policy distillation to MOPD, budget allocation, routing, and update protection. Matches the repository's eight-paper guide. AI-assisted editing.`

**Collection introduction / first note:** `The repository guide's eight-paper route: two classical multi-teacher foundations, two single-teacher OPD foundations, then four MOPD papers. This is a reading sequence, not a ranking or eight distinct MOPD methods. AI assistance was used for discovery and editing. Catalog and evidence: https://github.com/gyanchow/awesome-multi-teacher-distillation`

| Order | Paper and verified HF link | Role | Plain-text item note |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Distilling the Knowledge in a Neural Network](https://huggingface.co/papers/1503.02531) | Classical multi-teacher foundation | Start with soft targets, temperature, and ensemble compression. The catalog places this work in offline multi-teacher distillation; it does not establish an on-policy student rollout loop. |
| 2 | [Policy Distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/1511.06295) | Multi-task policy foundation | Examine how task-specific expert policies are consolidated into one student. Track who generates the training trajectories; the catalog places this work in offline multi-teacher distillation. |
| 3 | [Knowledge Distillation of Large Language Models (MiniLLM)](https://huggingface.co/papers/2306.08543) | Single-teacher foundation | Read for reverse-KL distillation on student-sampled behavior and stabilization. The catalog labels this MiniLLM; the HF page uses the shorter paper title. |
| 4 | [On-Policy Distillation of Language Models: Learning from Self-Generated Mistakes](https://huggingface.co/papers/2306.13649) | Single-teacher foundation | Focus on on-policy GKD's student-generated sequences and teacher feedback. The general framework also allows fixed-data mixtures and flexible divergences; it remains a single-teacher foundation. |
| 5 | [MOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Capability Integration in LLM Post-Training](https://huggingface.co/papers/2606.30406) | MOPD formulation | Independently train domain RL teachers, then consolidate their capabilities through direct supervision on student-generated trajectories. Use this as a reference formulation. |
| 6 | [Open-MOPD: Diagnosing and Fixing Capability Imbalance in Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.19098) | MOPD allocation | Examines capability imbalance and token-budget allocation. Compare token-share balancing, gap-aware allocation, and student-reward refresh with the reference MOPD setup. |
| 7 | [MOPD-Router: Rethinking Teacher Routing in Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.30837) | MOPD routing | Compares token-level routing across an expert pool. ExpertAlign relates specialization and teaching directions; check the rollout policy and sampled-token objective when comparing methods. |
| 8 | [PMOPD: Task Ordering, Cycling, and Parameter-Update Subspace Protection in Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.34605) | MOPD conflict control | Study task-update subspaces, ordering, and cycling. PMOPD protects task directions by projecting gradients and optimizer updates during multi-teacher reverse-KL training. |
