# Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation

> **Verification cutoff: 2026-08-31 (Asia/Shanghai).** This page is a primary-source-checked snapshot of the 21 `core_mopd` and 20 `mopd_systems` records in [`data/papers.json`](../data/papers.json). Entries are sorted by first public date, newest first.

This catalog uses a deliberately strict test: **(1)** the current or near-current student policy generates the trained trajectory/state, **(2)** at least two independently identifiable teachers, expert checkpoints, or teacher views supervise it, and **(3)** those signals directly update one student through a divergence, sampled-token advantage, field/feature match, or equivalent distillation objective.

Status labels:

- **Strict** — satisfies all three tests in a dedicated method or study.
- **Hybrid** — contains a genuine multi-teacher on-policy stage, but mixes it with offline/fixed-data continuation, supervised anchoring, or a separate RL objective in the reported recipe.
- **Analysis** — uses a qualifying MOPD setup primarily to diagnose or compare behavior rather than to introduce only a new training recipe.
- **System report** — MOPD is a material stage in a larger model/system pipeline; this label does not by itself claim a standalone MOPD-method contribution.

`RKL`, `FKL`, and `JSD` mean reverse KL, forward KL, and Jensen–Shannon divergence. `—` means that no author-linked code or project/model artifact was verified from the primary sources by the cutoff; it does not mean that no third-party implementation exists.

## Classification evidence for representative entries

The evidence below records the minimum method facts used for classification. It is intentionally paraphrased; follow the linked primary paper/report for equations and implementation details.

- **MiMo-V2-Flash — System report, strict MOPD stage.** The report explicitly names *Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation*: the student samples responses, a domain-matched RL teacher scores the sampled tokens with teacher/student log-probabilities, and that dense signal enters the student update alongside an outcome reward. The linked repository provides model/report artifacts, not a verified standalone MOPD trainer.
- **Baichuan-M3 — Hybrid system report.** Its recipe first performs fixed-data/offline clipped forward-KL distillation, then runs a mixed-domain online stage with reverse-KL teacher priors and task rewards. The latter is on-policy, but the published recipe is explicitly multi-stage; the report does not disclose the final teacher-routing/weight formula as fully as dedicated MOPD papers.
- **Nemotron-Cascade 2 — System report, strict MOPD stage.** For each domain, the strongest retained Cascade-RL checkpoint acts as teacher; the current student generates the trajectory, and teacher-versus-student log-probability differences on sampled actions form the dense policy-gradient/RKL signal. NVIDIA's NeMo RL repository documents the reusable MOPD implementation.
- **DeepSeek-V4 — System report, strict MOPD stage.** The report describes independently trained domain experts, context-relevance-based expert use, current-policy trajectories, and full-vocabulary reverse KL. The date above follows the first-public date shown on the primary arXiv record (**2026-04-26**) rather than inferring a month from the arXiv identifier.
- **MOPD — Strict.** The canonical formulation independently trains multiple domain RL teachers, generates trajectories with the current student, routes each prompt to the matching teacher, and directly minimizes reverse KL (or its sampled-token/top-$K$ estimator). It satisfies all three repository tests without relying on static teacher generations.
- **Open-MOPD — Strict.** The paper and official repository expose a three-teacher math/code/instruction-following pipeline in which SmolLM3-3B student tokens receive routed RKL-style supervision; token-share balancing, gap-aware allocation, and reward refresh change the training budget, not the on-policy source of states.
- **Every Coin Has Two Sides — Strict analysis.** Its controlled multi-teacher experiments use student-policy trajectories and routed teacher supervision, but the main contribution is the empirical finding that OPD generalization can transfer across domains while the chosen prompt/teacher mixture creates a capability seesaw.
- **When Top-K Misses the Decision — Hybrid analysis.** It directly audits a two-teacher MOPD system and restores decision-critical support, but the training mixture is reported as 80% current-student continuation and 20% dataset continuation with an SFT anchor; it is therefore not labeled unqualified strict-only training.
- **Consolidating RLVR Capabilities Across Domains — Strict analysis.** Its MOPD arm meets the rollout/teacher/objective tests, while the paper's central contribution is a controlled comparison against parameter merging and mixed-domain RL rather than presenting MOPD as the only method.

## Scope cautions

- **On-policy is about the trained states, not when logits are computed.** Online-computed teacher logits over a static corpus are still offline MTKD; student-induced trajectories or states are required here.
- **“Multi” must refer to supervision sources.** Multiple samples from one teacher, multiple student rollouts, ordinary MoE experts, or several rewards without teacher distributions do not by themselves qualify.
- **System artifacts are not automatically training code.** MiMo-V2-Flash and Baichuan-M3 repositories primarily expose models/report materials; Nemotron, Open-MOPD, UI-MOPD, H-OPD, and other links explicitly labeled `Code` provide a verified implementation or paper-linked codebase.
- **Image, flow, ASR, and protein entries are intentional.** They satisfy the same state-source, teacher-count, and direct-objective test even though the supervised object may be a velocity field, acoustic-token distribution, or protein policy rather than natural-language tokens.
