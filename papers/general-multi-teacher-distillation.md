# General Multi-Teacher Distillation

> **Verification cutoff: 2026-08-31.** This catalog covers representative ensemble-to-student and multi-teacher knowledge-distillation work outside the repository's strict Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD) scope.

The papers below generally train on a static corpus, teacher-generated data, cached or online-computed teacher logits, intermediate features, similarities, or rationales. They are **not strict MOPD** because their teacher signals do not directly update the student on trajectories or states generated by the current or near-current student policy. They provide the offline distillation foundations, teacher-fusion mechanisms, routing ideas, and conflict-resolution techniques that inform MOPD.

All bibliographic metadata was checked against a primary source: an official proceedings or publisher page, arXiv record, author project page, or paper-linked code repository. `—` means that no official implementation was verified from those sources by the cutoff date; it does not mean that no third-party reproduction exists.

## Topology and mechanism tags

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **`E→1`** | **Ensemble-to-one:** teacher members are first aggregated into an ensemble distribution or prediction that supervises one student. This is broad multi-teacher distillation, not necessarily direct teacher-wise supervision. |
| **`MT→1`** | **Multi-teacher-to-one:** at least two separately identifiable teachers directly contribute losses, logits, features, relations, or responses to one deployable student. |
| **`KA`** | **Knowledge amalgamation:** heterogeneous teachers cover different label spaces, tasks, modalities, or capabilities that are unified in one student. |
| **`R`** | **Routing or adaptive weighting:** teacher influence is selected or weighted by example, relation, token, feature, gradient, or student state. |
| **`GEN`** | **Generative supervision:** the student learns from teacher-generated answers, rationales, or chain-of-thought rather than only white-box logits. |
| **`SEQ`** | **Sequential or progressive teachers:** multiple teachers act in stages rather than being fused simultaneously; this is a boundary case with teacher-assistant distillation. |

## Scope boundaries and neighboring paradigms

The main tables require either a real teacher ensemble or at least two identifiable teacher models. The following paradigms are related but should remain outside this catalog's core count:

- **Online mutual learning:** [Deep Mutual Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2018/html/Zhang_Deep_Mutual_Learning_CVPR_2018_paper.html) trains peers bidirectionally from scratch without a fixed pretrained teacher pool or a required single final student.
- **On-the-fly ensemble teachers:** [Knowledge Distillation by On-the-Fly Native Ensemble](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/hash/94ef7214c4a90790186e255304f8fd1f-Abstract.html) synthesizes its teacher from jointly trained branches rather than independent pretrained teachers.
- **Teacher-assistant chains:** [Improved Knowledge Distillation via Teacher Assistant](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/5963) and [Densely Guided Knowledge Distillation Using Multiple Teacher Assistants](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Son_Densely_Guided_Knowledge_Distillation_Using_Multiple_Teacher_Assistants_ICCV_2021_paper.html) derive all assistants from one root teacher instead of combining independent knowledge sources.
- **Multi-headed ensemble distribution distillation:** [Hydra: Preserving Ensemble Diversity for Model Distillation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.04694) preserves member-level diversity in a multi-headed student and is better cataloged as an ensemble-distribution variant than as a conventional single-output `MT→1` method.
- **Self-distillation and generations:** born-again networks, snapshot distillation, and ordinary self-distillation reuse one model lineage rather than independent teachers.
- **MoE and model merging:** multiple experts, task heads, parameter averaging, or inference-time ensembles are not knowledge distillation unless teacher outputs, features, relations, or generated responses explicitly supervise a student.
- **Cross-modal KD:** a single full-modality teacher supervising a unimodal student is still single-teacher KD; multiple modality- or task-specific teachers are required for inclusion here.
